# Fixed 2020 household-income bands

Approved October 6, 2026 after inspecting the reviewed HRM distribution. Use the following fixed Canadian-dollar bands for median before-tax total household income in 2020: below $40,000; $40,000–<$60,000; $60,000–<$80,000; $80,000–<$100,000; $100,000–<$120,000; $120,000–<$160,000; $160,000 or more. Every finite lower bound is inclusive and every upper bound exclusive. Classification uses the reported figure without rounding.

The seven bands contain 13, 88, 147, 169, 98, 71, and 13 published HRM area medians respectively. They are readable dollar intervals rather than city-specific ranks; retain the same thresholds for other cities using the same measure and year. The broader upper intervals keep the sparse high-income tail useful. These counts describe area medians, not the distribution of individual households.

The authoritative configuration is `data/income-bands.json`. Its measure, year, and currency are validated during browser-data preparation. Use a sequential light-to-dark green/teal palette, with exact dollar labels and numerical values in the accessible area list. Unavailable income is a separate neutral grey category and remains selectable. Colour alone never supplies the income value.
