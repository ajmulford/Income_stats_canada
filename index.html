        Sources and how to read this map

        Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile and 2021 census geography. Fixed dollar bands use the same thresholds across comparable city maps. No inflation adjustment, wage projection, or additional income rounding has been applied.

        Values describe the households across each census area. They do not describe an individual home, individual income, or affordability. Income in 2020 may reflect pandemic-era changes and should not be read as current income. Confidentiality-suppressed figures appear as unavailable.

        Separately enumerated reserve subdivisions—Cole Harbour 30, Beaver Lake 17, Sheet Harbour 36, and Wallace Hills 14A—are outside the Halifax municipal census subdivision used here.

        Place lookup uses verified GeoSuite point records. Civic-community boundaries have unresolved consultation status, so search uses place points. Duplicate names show separate locations. A point identifies its containing census area, without assigning income to an entire community. Try a more specific name if the result is ambiguous; commonly used neighbourhood names may be absent.
